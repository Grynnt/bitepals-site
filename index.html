From photo to facts

The AI names the food. The database does the maths.

With your OK, the photo of your plate goes to the AI. It names what it sees, nothing more. Every number then comes from USDA FoodData Central, the public food database of the US Department of Agriculture.

  • Results are labelled as estimates.
  • Nothing is dropped silently. Foods we can't match are listed.
  • Your selfie never goes to the AI.
